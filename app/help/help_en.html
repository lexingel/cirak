What can Çırak do?

A tiny apprentice stirring a cauldron next to you while you work. Tell it what you're doing and now and then it asks a curious question; voice commands control your music and apps.

How to talk to it

  • Narrate as you work: "I'm lining up the menu buttons". The apprentice listens and asks a short question now and then. You pick how often in the menu.
  • Hold the key for a command: while Right Ctrl (changeable in the menu) is held, everything you say is a command. Works over music too.
  • When it's quiet: start with "Apprentice, …", no key needed.
  • Typing: click the apprentice, type, press Enter. Commands work typed too.

Commands

  • Music: "next song", "previous song", "pause", "play"
  • Volume: "volume up", "volume down", "mute", "unmute"
  • Open an app: "open Spotify", "launch Discord". Only apps on your list open (menu: "Apps I may open…").
  • Ask Claude (if Claude Code is installed): "ask Claude: how do I sort a list in Python?" The answer shows in the bubble.
  • Tell Claude: "tell Claude to fix the README". Claude Code opens with the task typed in; nothing is sent until you press Enter.

Right-click menu

  • Microphone on/off and which mic to use
  • Question frequency and Language (English / Türkçe)
  • Apprentice: pick a look (Wizard, Blacksmith, Healer, Scribe, Archer, Cook)
  • Question model: an Ollama model for better questions, with its size and trade-off next to it
  • Command key and Apps I may open

Drag the apprentice anywhere on the screen. It goes quiet by itself while you're in a call.

Privacy

What you say is turned into text on this PC and sent nowhere. The only things that leave it: the sentence after "ask Claude" (through your own Claude login), a "tell Claude" task (when you press Enter), and the model downloads on first use.
