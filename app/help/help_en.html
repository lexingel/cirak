What can Çırak do?

A tiny apprentice stirring a cauldron next to you while you work. Tell it what you're doing and now and then it asks a curious question; voice commands control your music and apps.

How to talk to it

  • Narrate as you work: "I'm lining up the menu buttons". The apprentice listens and asks a short question now and then. You pick how often in the menu.
  • Hold the key for a command: while Right Ctrl (changeable in the menu) is held, everything you say is a command. Works over music too.
  • When it's quiet: start with "Apprentice, …", no key needed.
  • Typing: click the apprentice (or right-click > Type to me), type, press Enter. For a command, start with /: "/next song", "/open Spotify". No microphone needed; with the mic off, "Hide for an hour" still works and it comes back by itself.

Commands

  • Getting started: "Apprentice, let's start" and it asks a different opening question each time ("What are we working on today?").
  • After a distraction: "Apprentice, where was I?" repeats the last two things you narrated. They live only in memory and are gone when you close it.
  • A big task: "Apprentice, break this down: clean the kitchen" splits it into 3-5 small steps. Needs the question model.
  • End of the day: "Apprentice, done for today" quotes three things you said today (first, middle, last), then forgets them. Nothing is saved.
  • Music: "next song", "previous song", "pause", "play"
  • Volume: "volume up", "volume down", "mute", "unmute"
  • Open an app: "open Spotify", "launch Discord". Only apps on your list open (menu: "Apps I may open…").
  • Ask Claude (if Claude Code is installed): "ask Claude: how do I sort a list in Python?" The answer shows in the bubble. (goes online: Anthropic)
  • Tell Claude: "tell Claude to fix the README". Claude Code opens with the task typed in; nothing is sent until you press Enter. (goes online after Enter)
  • Hide / come back: "Apprentice, hide" disappears for an hour, "Apprentice, come back" brings it back. Also in the menu.

Right-click menu

  • What can you do? opens this page
  • Turn mic off / on, Hide for an hour (only while it can hear you: "Apprentice, come back" is the way back)
  • Question frequency ("Never" just listens and asks nothing) and Language (English / Türkçe)
  • Settings:
    • Microphone: which mic to use
    • Apprentice: pick a look (Wizard, Blacksmith, Healer, Scribe, Archer, Cook)
    • Question model: an Ollama model for better questions, with its size and trade-off next to it
    • Apps I may open and Command key
    • Calm apprentice (on at first): stirs slowly and never throws in ingredients by itself. Untick it for a livelier apprentice.
    • Listen during fullscreen (on at first): keeps listening while a game or video hides the apprentice. Untick it and the mic is off until the game leaves fullscreen.
    • Mod folder: add your own questions (questions\en\*.txt, one per line) and your own looks (looks\<name>\). The README.txt in the folder explains how.

Drag the apprentice anywhere on the screen. It goes quiet by itself while you're in a call and steps aside when a game or video goes fullscreen on its screen (see "Listen during fullscreen").

The small dot by the cauldron shows the mic, by colour and by shape:
● green with a dot: listening   ❚❚ yellow with bars: paused for a call   ✕ red with an X: off or no mic   – grey with a dash: the listener isn't running (the top menu line says so).

As you talk, the little jar left of the apprentice slowly fills. When it's full it moves to the shelf on the right and a new colour starts. It never empties, with no numbers or streaks; days off cost nothing.

Privacy

What you say is turned into text on this PC and sent nowhere. The only things that leave it: the sentence after "ask Claude" (through your own Claude login), a "tell Claude" task (when you press Enter), and the model downloads on first use. Claude's last full answer is kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Cirak\last_answer.txt, so a long one can be read in full.
